## Design Context

### Users
University students at RV University (members of the Poéthra literary club). They check the site for personal recognition, a sense of belonging, and gentle motivation (literary streaks). The context is informal, intimate, and meaningful.

### Brand Personality
**Warm. Eloquent. Timeless.**
Intimate, grounded, and nostalgic. It feels like an indie bookstore—safe, curated, and alive. It avoids the "coldness" of modern SaaS and the "loudness" of gaming dashboards.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Indie Bookstore & Literary Magazine**
- **Theme**: Equally intentional Light and Dark modes. Light mode is "Sunday morning by a window"; Dark mode is "midnight writing by lamplight."
- **Color Palette**: 
  - **Light**: Warm Cream / Aged Parchment (#F5ECD7) background.
  - **Dark**: Ink Blue (#1B2A4A) depth instead of gray.
  - **Accents**: Oxblood / Deep Burgundy (#6B1C2A) for badges, streaks, and highlights.
- **Micro-details**: Parchment textures, ink-stroke borders, serif-dominant typography.

### Design Principles
1. **Curate, Don't Calculate**: The data should feel like entries in a yearbook or an archive, not entries in a spreadsheet.
2. **Warmth Over Precision**: Prioritize a "handmade" or "physical" feel (paper textures, soft shadows) over pixel-perfect industrial clean-lines.
3. **Belonging Over Battle**: Rewards participation and streaks as much as points. Gentle competition vs. aggressive ranking.
4. **Timelessness**: Use typography and layout that could belong to a 1920s magazine or a modern indie journal.
